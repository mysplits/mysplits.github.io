It reads before it asks.

Thresholds, zones, races, fitness and your last weeks of training come from TrainingPeaks. You only answer what it cannot see there.

A plan to your race.

Base, build, peak and taper counted back from race day, three load weeks for each recovery week, and every session in your calendar, ready for your watch.

A coach that says no.

Built to give you the best answer for your race, not the one you want to hear. When you push for an extra hard day in a recovery week, it explains why not.

Setup, inside Claude

No forms. You set it up by talking to Claude, once.

Type “Set up SPLITS” and Claude walks you through six steps. Pick one to see how it goes.

    Get it

    Three steps and your first week is in your calendar.

    1. 1

      Download the skill

      A small zip with SPLITS's method and instructions. Download splits.zip

    2. 2

      Add it to Claude

      Upload it in Settings, Capabilities, Skills. Claude learns SPLITS and waits until you ask.

    3. 3

      Start the setup

      In the Code tab of Claude Desktop, type Set up SPLITS. Claude connects your TrainingPeaks for you and takes it from there.

    You need

    • Claude Desktop with the Code tab
    • A TrainingPeaks account (Premium to plan ahead and sync structured workouts)
    • Python 3.10 or newer. Claude helps you install it if it is missing

    Your privacy

    • No SPLITS server: your own Claude talks to TrainingPeaks from your computer
    • Your TrainingPeaks session is stored encrypted on your machine and never shown to Claude
    • Nothing goes into your calendar without your OK

    SPLITS is a training tool, not a doctor. If something hurts, if you feel dizzy or faint, or if you have a health condition, talk to a medical professional before following any plan. It connects through the open-source TrainingPeaks MCP server by James McAulay and is not affiliated with TrainingPeaks.
